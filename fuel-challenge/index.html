Palma 30×30 Fuel Challengeeat well. move more. feel good

Fuel your day with protein-rich dishes, wholesome meals and feel-good drinks, created for Dubai Fitness Challenge. Order from our Fitness Menu at Golden Mile or Creek Harbour, collect stamps and unlock rewards.

See the Fitness Menu How it works

How it worksevery healthy choice deserves a little reward

Order anything from our Fitness Menu, collect a stamp with every order and unlock a new reward along the way. Valid at both cafés from 31 October to 29 November.

  1. 5 First boost A complimentary protein shake on your next visit
  2. 10 Power up A complimentary Fitness Menu course on your next visit
  3. 15 Full charge AED 500 on your Palma Rewards account
  4. 25 Palma champion AED 1000 on your Palma Rewards account

Rewards are for Fitness Menu orders during the challenge. Ask the team about your Palma Rewards account.
