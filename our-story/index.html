Since 2022 · Palm Jumeirah & Dubai Creek Harbour

Our storya slower rhythm in a fast-paced city

European café culture with a Mediterranean warm touch. A place to slow down, eat well and meet the people who matter, opened on Palm Jumeirah in 2022 and at Dubai Creek Harbour in 2023.

Visit our cafés See the menu

Our philosophya slower rhythm in a fast-paced city

Café de Palma is a place for people. For those who value a slower rhythm in a fast-paced city. For those seeking inner balance, honest natural food and a warm atmosphere.

Guests come for flavours, conversations, moments with themselves, new meetings, books or work. Cultures, stories and perspectives meet here, and everyone feels at home.

Here, life pauses — but never stops.

What we care about

  • 01Care for ourselves, for others

    Care isn’t a marketing tool. It’s the heart of our culture: from the kitchen to the table, from the team to the interior.

  • 02Natural and wholesome

    Carefully selected organic ingredients, natural materials and honest recipes, made with care for the person we serve.

  • 03Friendliness

    We believe in the power of genuine connection, and we want every guest to feel truly welcome and at home.

  • 04Aesthetics in everyday life

    Beauty lives in the everyday: in how we set the table, in the little details, in the food we serve and the moments we hold on to.

  • 05Honesty & transparency

    We are open about who we are, what we cook with and how we work, because trust begins with sincerity.
