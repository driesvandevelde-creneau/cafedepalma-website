Good to knowabout our food

  • Breakfast until 4 pm

    Eggs, toasts, Benedicts on our 48-hour fermented flatbread, pancakes, crêpes and bowls, served until 4 pm.

  • Main menu from 12 pm

    Salads, soups, pasta, mains and pizza from the oven, with desserts and Italian gelato to finish.

  • Gluten & sugar-free

    Gluten-free and sugar-free choices from breakfast to dessert, and gluten-free pasta and toast on request.

  • Vegetarian & lactose-free

    Vegetarian dishes throughout, and lactose-free options in both food and drinks.

  • Kids menu

    Smaller portions and gentle flavours for little foodies, with gluten-free options.

  • Allergies

    We kindly ask you to inform us about any allergies or food intolerances before ordering. Allergens are marked on every menu.
